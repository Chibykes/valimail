Five ways to say verified.

Filled marks, not UI icons. Indigo tile, mint only where it means “valid.” Each one is shown big, at 32px, at 16px, and in a header lockup.

01 · Apex

Monogram

The letter V, nothing else. Quiet, heavy, still a V at 16px. The name does the explaining.

Valimail

02 · Halve

Two-tone V

Same V, split down the stem. White is the name. Mint is the verdict. Most ownable of the set.

Valimail

03 · Flap

Mail

Envelope reduced to the flap. Mint seal on the fold. Says mail without the usual rectangle-and-badge.

Valimail

04 · Atmark

Symbol

The @ is the product. Mint check in the counter. Most literal “this address is real.” Needs 24px or more.

Valimail

05 · Signal

Deliverability

Three bars, the last one mint. List health went valid. Less “mail,” more “result.” Holds up at 16px.

Valimail

Now

In the app

Envelope plus a corner badge. Fine up close. The badge collapses at favicon size, and the shape could belong to any mail app.

My pick: Halve. Apex if you want quieter. Atmark if the symbol should say “email” before the word does.
